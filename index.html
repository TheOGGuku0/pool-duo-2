A QUICK TABLE TALK

Find your angle.

  1. Place & aim

    Click to place the cue ball when you have ball in hand. Move your mouse or drag on the table to aim. Use ← / →, the wheel, or precision buttons for fine adjustments.

  2. Call & shoot

    After the break, select your ball and pocket, or tap them on the table. “From aim” suggests a call. Drag the power meter and release to shoot. Keyboard: focus the meter, adjust with arrows, then press Enter.

  3. Make it your shot

    Drag the dot on the cue ball for follow, draw, and side spin. Enable spin-aware prediction in Settings to preview the effect.

  4. Finish the rack

    Your first legally called pocket assigns solids or stripes. Clear your group, then call and pocket the 8. A scratch, wrong first contact, or no rail after contact gives ball in hand. A safety always passes the turn.

WPA-style called-shot rules, including break choices. First break goes to Player 1; rematches alternate. This is a planar simulation: jump and massé shots are not modeled.
